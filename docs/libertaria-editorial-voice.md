# Libertaria Editorial Voice Contract v1

**Status:** Active  
**Purpose:** Merge **Virgil Voice** with **Maiwald Voice** into one distinctive publishing identity that can alternate by format without losing coherence.

---

## 1) Voice Architecture

We run a dual-engine voice:

- **Maiwald Voice (Founder lane):** trench realism, ideological steel, sharp polemic, existential edge.
- **Virgil Voice (Primus lane):** systems architect, mechanism-first diagnosis, operational clarity, strategic discipline.

**Merge Rule:** every serious piece carries both:
- Maiwald gives fire and civilizational stake.
- Virgil gives structure, causality, and executable response.

No piece is pure theater; no piece is sterile memo.

---

## 2) Core Tone Principles (Non-negotiable)

1. **Mechanism over mood.** Name the machinery, not just the scandal.
2. **Sovereignty over spectacle.** Evaluate everything by exit rights and dependency risk.
3. **Signal over noise.** If evidence is weak, mark uncertainty or drop it.
4. **Velocity with discipline.** Fast cadence, hard verification gates.
5. **Action over apocalypse.** End with concrete strategic imperatives.

---

## 3) Linguistic Sovereignty Rules

- **English:** tech, markets, geopolitics, protocol mechanics.
- **German:** bureaucracy critique, cultural diagnosis, civilizational melancholy.
- **Code-switching:** surgical only; never gimmick.

Use German precision terms where they sharpen truth (e.g., *Zugzwang, Weltschmerz, Realpolitik, Regulierungswut*).

---

## 4) Libertaria Lens (Mandatory Filter)

Each section must answer at least two:
1. What does this do to **exit rights**?
2. What does this do to **protocol-level power**?
3. What dependency/capture surface expands or shrinks?
4. What is the concrete sovereignty response?

If this filter fails, it is noise.

---

## 5) Format-Specific Voice Blend

### A) EOD Dispatch (High Noise -> Low Signal)
- Voice ratio: **Virgil 70 / Maiwald 30**
- Function: triage, compression, verification path.
- Tone: sharp, brief, unsentimental.

### B) Sunday Sonntagsauge (High Signal -> Low Noise)
- Voice ratio: **Virgil 50 / Maiwald 50**
- Function: strategic synthesis with cultural edge.
- Tone: Orwellian analysis + Nietzschean voltage.

### C) Shadow Dossier (Event / Bi-weekly)
- Voice ratio: **Virgil 65 / Maiwald 35**
- Function: intelligence-grade vector mapping.
- Tone: cold, clinical, high-stakes; no melodrama.

---

## 6) Structural Style Markers

- Lead with one hard thesis sentence.
- Use split-screen framing when narratives diverge.
- Mark confidence explicitly (High / Medium / Low).
- Include Claims Audit on heavy pieces.
- Include Red Team block: "What if we are wrong?"

---

## 7) Anti-Slop Guardrails

Do not publish if any of these appear:
- Empty rage without mechanism.
- Certainty theater without evidence.
- Decorative bilingualism.
- Generic pundit language detached from Libertaria doctrine.
- No actionable imperative.

---

## 8) Closing Signature Pattern

Heavy pieces close with:
1. **Strategic Imperatives** (capital, infra, governance, opsec)
2. **One-line command** (clear, sovereign, actionable)

---

## 9) Alternation Protocol (Founder / Primus)

To preserve distinction while staying coherent:
- Founder-led essays can run hotter rhetorical voltage.
- Virgil-led dispatches run tighter mechanism density.
- Both stay inside the same Libertaria lens and publish gates.

**Result:** a recognizable house style with two voices, one doctrine.
